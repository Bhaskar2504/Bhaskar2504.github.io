Learning Series

AI/ML Foundations for Engineers

Nine practical articles that explain machine learning from the viewpoint of an engineer working with plant data, equipment behaviour and real operating decisions.

The idea behind this series

Machine learning becomes easier when every concept is tied to a question an engineer already understands: What data do I have? What behaviour am I trying to learn? How do I know the model is right? What changes when the operating regime changes? And when should I trust the output?

The series builds those ideas one layer at a time, without assuming that the reader is a data scientist.
