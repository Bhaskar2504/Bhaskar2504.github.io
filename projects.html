Reliability · Maintenance Data · APM

Maintenance records are engineering evidence—not just work history.

The framework reconstructs asset histories, links precursor chains, tests repair effectiveness, compares sister assets, detects common-mode patterns and identifies hierarchy/data-quality failures that can distort APM analytics.

Working principles.

Evidence first. Engineering context explicit. Uncertainty preserved.

Problem framingStart from the engineering decisionDefine system, operating context and decision before selecting the method.
EvidenceTest supporting and contradicting signalsAvoid one-signal conclusions and make assumptions visible.
Data qualityHierarchy and sensor credibility are part of diagnosisBad data architecture can create false histories before modelling begins.
GovernanceKeep human verification explicitAnalytics and AI inform the engineering decision; they do not replace it.
