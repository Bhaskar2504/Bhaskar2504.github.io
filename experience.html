Experience

From plant operations to Industrial AI.

A career built by moving progressively closer to the question of how engineering decisions can be improved with better data, models and systems.

My experience spans operating and commissioning large thermal power assets, plant performance and commercial functions, process modelling and industrial analytics, and end-to-end Industrial AI implementation.

The roles are different, but each added another layer: how the plant operates, how performance is measured, how business decisions depend on plant behaviour, how data represents that behaviour, and how AI can support engineering decisions at scale.

Career pattern

Operations → Performance → Analytics → Industrial AI

The progression matters because modern industrial AI sits on top of engineering context, data quality, equipment behaviour and user adoption—not just model accuracy.
