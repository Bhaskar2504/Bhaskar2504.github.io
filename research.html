Research & Education

Technical foundation and continuous learning.

Electrical engineering, power systems, plant operations, data analysis and machine learning form the technical base behind my industrial work.

My formal education is in Electrical and Electronics Engineering, followed by postgraduate study in the same discipline and specialized power-plant training. Professional experience then extended that foundation into thermal power operations, plant performance, process analytics, reliability and Industrial AI.

The learning approach on this site follows the same pattern: understand the engineering principle, connect it to data, and then examine where modern analytics or AI can improve the decision.

Learning philosophy

Engineering fundamentals first; tools second.

Algorithms, programming languages and platforms change quickly. Thermodynamics, electrical behaviour, rotating-equipment physics, measurement quality and sound reasoning remain the foundation.
