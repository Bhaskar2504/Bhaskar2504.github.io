Measurement / instrumentation anomaly

The isolated nature of the signal makes sensor or circuit error plausible, but it does not prove the equipment is healthy.

1Compare redundant or nearby temperature channels.
2Inspect RTD wiring, terminals and transmitter path.
3Cross-check with an independent measurement if possible.
4Confirm load and cooling context did not change.

Key ideaContradicting evidence is valuable. A good diagnostic system should know when the rest of the machine does not agree with one sensor.
Scope: A single deviating RTD suggests an instrumentation hypothesis; it does not by itself prove the absence of a real localized thermal issue.
