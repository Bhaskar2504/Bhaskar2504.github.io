When pressure and temperature fall together.

The important clue was not either signal alone. It was that both changed together at the same operating moment.

Changing valve / bypass flow path

A valve drifting away from its intended position can change hydraulic resistance and heat removal together.

1Compare valve command and actual feedback.
2Check loop flow and differential pressure.
3Inspect bypass / manual valve alignment.

Key ideaCorrelation becomes useful when two sensors describe the same physical mechanism—not merely because they moved at the same time.
