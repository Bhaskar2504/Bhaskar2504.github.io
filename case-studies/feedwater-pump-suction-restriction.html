Suction-side restriction

A degrading suction path can shift the pump operating point and reduce hydraulic performance. Electrical loading is supporting evidence, not standalone proof.

1Confirm strainer / suction differential pressure.
2Check suction pressure and available NPSH margin.
3Compare flow, head and speed at the same operating state.
4Use motor current/power only with operating-point context.
01Restriction buildsSuction pressure loss increases.
02Operating point shiftsPump sees poorer inlet conditions.

Key ideaA signal can belong to one subsystem and be caused by another. Good diagnostics follow the physics across subsystem boundaries.
Technical caution: Motor current does not have a universal direction under suction restriction; it depends on the pump curve, system curve, speed and resulting operating point.
