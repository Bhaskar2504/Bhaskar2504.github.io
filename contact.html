Industrial AI. Reliability. Asset Performance.

For professional discussions around industrial engineering, predictive maintenance, APM, plant analytics or Industrial AI, choose the most useful way to connect.

I am most interested in conversations where plant-domain knowledge, reliability, data and AI have to work together. That can mean solving a technical problem, discussing an implementation challenge, exchanging ideas on industrial AI, or exploring a professional opportunity.

If useful, include the asset or problem context in your message so the discussion can start from the engineering question rather than from generic AI terminology.

Best starting point

Describe the engineering problem in one or two sentences.

Asset, operating context, what is changing, and what decision you are trying to improve are usually enough to begin a useful conversation.

Before You Write

A little context makes technical conversations much better.

What system?

Pump, turbine, boiler, motor, process loop, data platform, reliability workflow or another industrial system.

What problem?

Failure detection, false alerts, performance degradation, data quality, model validation, implementation or another specific need.

What outcome?

Earlier warning, better diagnosis, clearer KPI, more reliable calculation, improved workflow or another engineering decision.
